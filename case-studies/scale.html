Marketplace platform

Scale

Designing a smarter way to buy and sell businesses.

An AI-powered marketplace that helps buyers, sellers, and funders make more informed decisions through business data, financial insights, and AI.

My role

Lead Product Designer

Timeline

4 months

Platform

Web application

UX strategy · Research · Workshops · Interaction & UI design

The challenge

Buying or selling a business is a decision built on uncertainty.

The project started with a one-page client brief for an intelligent business marketplace. My first responsibility wasn't designing screens — it was understanding what the product needed to solve.

Through stakeholder interviews and workshops, I explored the business vision, user needs, transaction journey, trust challenges, financial evaluation, and opportunities for AI.

“How might we help users make more confident decisions when buying or selling a business?”

My role

From defining the problem to shaping the product.

I led the project from stakeholder discovery and workshops through research, product strategy, UX architecture, interaction design, AI thinking, and visual design.

Research

We studied the market before defining the experience.

I conducted a detailed UX audit of Flippa, Smergers, Empire Flippers, SourceScrub, and Boopos. Rather than looking only at visual inspiration, we analysed how these products handled five parts of the journey.

Discover

Search · Filters · Recommendations

Evaluate

Financials · Valuation · Insights

Trust

Verification · NDA · Escrow

Communicate

Messaging · Notifications · Support

Transact

Offers · Payments · Funding

Competitive analysis became a tool for understanding the category, not simply collecting inspiration.

The key insight

Finding a business wasn't the hardest problem.

Understanding whether it was worth pursuing was.

This shifted our focus from simply building a marketplace to helping users evaluate opportunities and build confidence throughout the decision journey.

UX principle

The core UX problem was confidence.

Discover

Is this relevant?

→

Evaluate

Does it look credible?

→

Understand

What does the data tell me?

→

Validate

→

We designed the experience to progressively increase confidence at every stage of the journey.

User experience

One product, multiple users.

Buyer

Find businesses, evaluate opportunities, understand financial performance and make informed acquisition decisions.

Seller

List a business, control information visibility, manage buyer requests and communicate with interested buyers.

Funder

Review financial assistance requests and evaluate opportunities.

Admin

Manage users, listings, loans, commissions and platform performance.

Discover→Evaluate→Request access→Analyse→Validate→Connect→Fund→Decide

AI & product thinking

AI wasn't the feature. Better decisions were.

AI valuation

Understand potential business value.

Predictive insights

Explore potential revenue, expenses and profitability.

ROI & financial analysis

Understand potential returns and investment scenarios.

Generative AI

Assist with business information and analysis.

Valuation, prediction, risk and ROI presented as answers to a decision rather than as AI features.

“The goal wasn't to make the product AI-powered. The goal was to make difficult decisions easier.”

Final experience

From a marketplace to a connected decision journey.

Outcome

Turning a complex marketplace into a decision platform.

The final product connected business discovery, financial intelligence, AI-driven insights, communication, and funding into a single experience.

The client was pleased with the final product and felt it exceeded expectations in customer experience.

Intended business goal

An intended business goal was to improve transaction conversion by at least 5%.

Reflection

What Scale taught me.

01

Research can shape the product, not just the interface.

02

03
