Mobile commerce

Tanishq

Reimagining a premium jewellery shopping experience for mobile.

A mobile app revamp focused on making product discovery, evaluation and checkout more intuitive while creating a consistent digital experience for an established enterprise brand.

My role

Lead Product Designer

Platform

Mobile app

UX research · Competitive analysis · User flows · Wireframes · Visual design

The challenge

A premium shopping experience needs more than beautiful products.

Customers browsing jewellery need to discover products, compare options, understand details, check availability, evaluate price and finally complete a purchase.

The opportunity was to make these interactions feel more connected, intuitive and e-commerce friendly.

My role

I worked across the experience from research to final design.

Research

We looked at how leading e-commerce products solved common shopping problems.

For each feature, we compared competitor approaches, noting strengths and weaknesses.

Six competitors compared feature by feature across search, listing, filters and product detail.

A heuristic pass over the existing app — every observation tied to a specific frame.

From research to experience

The focus was on making key shopping moments easier.

Discovery

Make search and browsing more useful.

Evaluation

Make product information easier to understand.

Interaction

Support actions such as wishlist, size selection and Try On.

Purchase

Create a clearer cart and checkout journey.

The largest experience changes were focused around the product description page and checkout, with the goal of making them more intuitive and e-commerce friendly.

A more connected mobile shopping experience.

The redesign brought together competitive research, refined shopping journeys, improved PDP and checkout experiences, and a consistent visual foundation.

The project progressed through design reviews, iteration and developer handoff.

Reflection

Making the journey feel natural.

The goal wasn't simply to make Tanishq look more premium on mobile. It was to make the journey from discovery to purchase feel more natural.
